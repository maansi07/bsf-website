Sport changes what a child believes is possible.

Baptist Sports Foundation was established in 2024 in Mangaluru. We promote grassroots sports, nurture emerging talent and use sport as a tool for empowerment and positive social change.

Here are the barriers we work on.

  • Limited access

    Rural and government school students have fewer facilities and opportunities.

  • No equipment

    Rackets, shoes and kit are often unaffordable.

  • Limited coaching

    Quality training is not available to everyone.

  • Talent without support

    Promising athletes lack encouragement and resources to progress.

  • Low sports science awareness

    Fitness, nutrition and scientific training are rarely taught.

  • Fading active habits

    Children need to love moving, from an early age.

Scholarships built on memory

Two scholarships carry the names of people who gave their lives to sport.

B

Preetish Latheesh Salian Memorial Sports Scholarship

Established in memory of Preetish Latheesh Salian, an exceptionally talented young badminton player who courageously battled cancer. The scholarship keeps his passion for badminton alive by paying coaching fees for deserving young athletes, so they can pursue their dreams and grow in the sport.

N

Nagesh A. Memorial Chinnara Sports Scholarship

Established in memory of Late Nagesh A., a dedicated Physical Education teacher who devoted his life to nurturing young sporting talent. The scholarship recognises students from government schools who have shown excellence in sports at state and national levels, and supports them in pursuing their sporting aspirations.
